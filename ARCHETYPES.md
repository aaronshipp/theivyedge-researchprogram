# Ivy Edge Research Archetypes

This document lists all 8 research archetypes, their descriptions, fields, project concepts, and how they are determined.

---

## How Archetypes Are Determined

Archetypes are derived from two quiz answers:

- **Q1 — "Choose your instrument of change"** (Tool): Biology, CS/Engineering, Humanities, or Creative Media
- **Q2 — "How do you solve problems?"** (Scale): Micro or Macro

| Tool | Micro | Macro |
|------|-------|-------|
| Biology / Life Sciences | The Bio-Innovator | The Ecological Architect |
| CS / Engineering | The Systems Architect | The Platform Builder |
| Humanities / Law / Policy | The Cultural Analyst | The Justice Engineer |
| Creative Arts / Media | The Narrative Scientist | The Impact Producer |

Additional quiz answers modify the result:

- **Q3 — Outcome** (paper / prototype / media / policy): Selects the project concept
- **Q4 — Crew** (independent / collaborative): Modifies description text
- **Q5 — Fuel** (curiosity / impact): Modifies description text

## Description Modifiers (Q4 x Q5)

The final result description appends one of these modifier phrases based on the user's Crew (Q4) and Fuel (Q5) answers:

| Crew | Fuel | Modifier |
|------|------|----------|
| Independent | Curiosity | You thrive in deep, solo exploration — driven by the thrill of uncovering what no one else has found. |
| Independent | Impact | You work best with laser focus — one researcher, one mission, one outcome that changes the game. |
| Collaborative | Curiosity | You believe the best discoveries happen at the intersection of minds — your curiosity multiplies in conversation. |
| Collaborative | Impact | You build coalitions. Your research is not just rigorous — it is designed to move people and reshape systems. |

## Gap Hook Prefixes (Q4 x Q5)

A "gap hook" is constructed from the modifier + the archetype's fields + university names to create a CTA:

| Crew | Fuel | Gap Hook Prefix |
|------|------|-----------------|
| Independent | Curiosity | To go deeper, you need a mentor who has navigated the frontiers of... |
| Independent | Impact | To turn your vision into reality, you need a mentor with hands-on experience in... |
| Collaborative | Curiosity | To build the right team, you need a mentor embedded in the research networks of... |
| Collaborative | Impact | To scale your impact, you need a mentor who knows how to mobilize resources in... |

The full gap hook follows the pattern: `{prefix} {field1} & {field2} & {field3} at Harvard, Columbia, Yale.`
